🔥 The Taste of You

Some flavors you never forget.
by Walusimbi Leon (SGSS)

About

The Taste of You is a sweeping international romance about Nadia, a Lebanese chef in Paris, and Ethan, an American journalist learning to feel again. They meet on a rainy Parisian sidewalk, and what begins as a seven-day affair becomes a journey across continents — from the cafés of the Left Bank to the cliffs of the Amalfi Coast, from the rooftop parties of Beirut to the kitchens of Brooklyn. It is a story about food and desire, about the architecture of intimacy, and about two people who discover that love is not a destination but a practice — something you build, meal by meal, night by night, in the space between two hearts.
