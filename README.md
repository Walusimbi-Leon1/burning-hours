## 📖 About

*The Taste of You* is a sweeping international romance about Nadia, a Lebanese chef in Paris, and Ethan, an American journalist learning to feel again. They meet on a rainy Parisian sidewalk, and what begins as a seven-day affair becomes a journey across continents — from the cafés of the Left Bank to the cliffs of the Amalfi Coast, from the rooftop parties of Beirut to the kitchens of Brooklyn.

A story about food and desire, about the architecture of intimacy, and about two people who discover that love is not a destination but a practice.
